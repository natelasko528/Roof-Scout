# Roof Scout Competitive Analysis
**Date**: November 1, 2025

## Executive Summary

Based on research of the roofing/roof inspection industry, here are the three key competitors and their features:

---

## 1. HAIL RECON (Interactive Hail Maps)
**Website**: interactivehailmaps.com  
**Focus**: Hail damage detection and insurance claims

### Core Features:
- ✅ Hail storm tracking and mapping
- ✅ Property-level hail damage assessment
- ✅ Insurance claim integration
- ✅ Historical hail data
- ✅ Automated damage scoring
- ✅ Photo-based damage detection
- ✅ PDF report generation
- ✅ API for third-party integrations

### Technology:
- AI-powered image analysis
- Satellite imagery integration
- Weather data APIs
- GIS mapping
- Mobile app (iOS/Android)

### Pricing: 
- Enterprise: Custom pricing
- Individual: $49-$199/month

### Strengths:
- Strong insurance industry focus
- Comprehensive hail database
- Good API ecosystem

### Weaknesses:
- Limited to hail damage only
- No general roof inspection features
- Expensive for small contractors

## Competitive Positioning

### Current Roof Scout Position:
- ✅ Lead management (good foundation)
- ✅ Basic AI integration (needs improvement)
- ⚠️ Map features (partial, missing markers)
- ❌ Weather data integration
- ❌ Drone support
- ❌ 3D modeling
- ❌ Professional reports
- ❌ Mobile app
- ❌ API access

### Market Opportunity:
The roofing software market is **fragmented** with:
- Hail Recon: Insurance focus
- Hail Trace: Weather focus  
- Roofr: Enterprise/contractor focus

**Roof Scout can differentiate** by being:
- **Canvassing-focused** (door-to-door sales)
- **Mobile-first** for field sales
- **AI-powered** lead scoring
- **Weather-integrated** for storm chasing
- **Affordable** for small contractors
